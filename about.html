Our Heritage & Vision

The Art House Sanctuary

A boutique destination in Luanda where Angolan contemporary art, peaceful architecture, and personal care converge into a quiet oasis.

Living Gallery

Featuring curated contemporary works by Angolan masters in every hallway and suite.

An Architectural Refuge

Rooted in Angolan Culture & Elegance

Art House was envisioned as an intimate alternative to conventional city hotels in Luanda. Crafted with warm stone textures, teak woodwork, and lush tropical greenery, it offers a serene retreat where guests can unwind from the rhythm of the capital.

Our passion lies in fostering genuine connection: between the guest and Angolan artistic heritage, between modern comfort and natural tranquility.

Curated Art Sanctuary

We collaborate directly with contemporary Angolan painters, sculptors, and photographers. Our rotating collection gives guests an authentic window into Angola's vibrant creative scene.

Botanical Courtyard

The center of Art House is our private tropical courtyard—a quiet garden filled with native palms, ambient water fountains, and shaded teak decks for morning coffee or evening reading.

Intuitive Concierge Care

Our local team provides discreet, thoughtful service—from arranging airport transfers at LAD Airport to organizing private gallery walkthroughs and city itineraries.
