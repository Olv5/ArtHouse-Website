Experience the soul of Luanda

Through Art, Culture and Genuine Hospitality

Set along the coast of Ilha do Cabo, in the heart of Luanda's entertainment district, Thomson Art House is an intimate 16-room boutique hotel where Angolan creativity and genuine hospitality come together.

Art is at the heart of who we are. From the spaces around you to the stories behind them, we celebrate the artists, culture, and character that make Angola unique—and we love sharing that with the people who come through our doors.

Our team is proudly Angolan and fluent in English, making it easy for guests from around the world to feel understood, welcomed, and at home from the moment they arrive.

Whether you're discovering Luanda for the first time or returning to a city you already know, Art House gives you a comfortable place to stay, a friendly community to connect with, and a local perspective on the best of Angola.

Come stay with us and experience Luanda at its best.
